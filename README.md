# Retail Sales Analysis

## Objective
Analyse retail sales data to uncover trends and provide actionable business insights.

## Tools Used
Python (pandas), Excel

## Dataset
Superstore retail dataset containing orders, sales, and customer data.

## Key Insights
- Technology category generates the highest revenue
- Sales consistently peak during Q4, indicating strong seasonal demand
- Western region outperforms other regions in total sales
- A small number of products contribute disproportionately to revenue

## Business Recommendations
- Increase inventory for high-performing categories before Q4
- Focus marketing efforts on underperforming regions
- Prioritise top-selling products to maximise revenue

## Outcome
Delivered data-driven insights that could support inventory planning, regional strategy, and revenue optimisation.
